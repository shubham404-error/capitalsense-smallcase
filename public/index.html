The idea

India is entering a different kind of growth cycle.

For years, investors could understand India's growth through familiar categories. Banks. IT. Consumer. Infrastructure. The next phase is more fragmented.

A data centre needs servers, but it also needs transformers, cooling, power distribution and transmission.

A solar project needs panels, but increasingly it also needs domestic cells, modules and a deeper supply chain.

An aircraft needs an engine, but it also needs hundreds of precision components manufactured to extremely demanding standards.

A defence order is not just a government announcement. It has to become radar, electronics, missiles, communications systems and platforms.

The interesting opportunities are often one layer behind the headline.
